Your profile

Five quick details. We use them to calculate your daily energy and macro targets.

What happens next

You will see your targets first, then a day of meals matched to them. You can edit your profile at any time.

Why these details

Age, height, weight and sex feed the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which estimates the calories your body uses at rest. Activity level scales that up, and your goal sets the surplus or deficit. The sex field is only used for that equation.

For adults aged 18 to 80. If you have a medical condition or are pregnant, talk to a clinician or dietitian instead.
